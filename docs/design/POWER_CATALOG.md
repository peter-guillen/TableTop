# RPGHub Power Catalog

Brainstorm catalog of powers: spells, abilities, techniques and traits. **Everything here is a draft idea.** It hasn't been balanced or seeded yet. The rules it's written against live in [GAME_RULES.md](GAME_RULES.md), and the item side is in [ITEM_CATALOG.md](ITEM_CATALOG.md).

- Every power has an **ID** (for example `MAN-03`) so we can point at it during balancing. IDs never get reused. If a power is cut, strike it through instead of deleting it.
- Questions that block a design are tagged `[PQ-n]` and listed in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md). Balancing findings are in [BALANCE_POWERS.md](BALANCE_POWERS.md).

### Columns

| Column           | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tier**         | T0 = at-will (no cost, unlimited). T1–T4 = the tier table above.                                                                                                                             |
| **Act**          | Major, Minor or Reaction.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Cost**         | `0`, `3 MP` or `4 HP`. Costed tier 3–4 techniques have no cost and use a recharge instead (**SR** or **LR**). Momentum never pays for a power (see guardrails).                              |
| **Range / Area** | Range in ft, then the shape and size. "Self" or "Touch" where it fits.                                                                                                                       |
| **Roll**         | **Atk** = d20 + Accuracy vs. 10 + Evasion (single-target damage). **Save** = target's d20 + Resolve vs. 11 + Dominance (areas, control, debuffs). **—** = no roll (buffs, healing, utility). |
| **Effect**       | Damage adds Might. Healing doesn't. A save means no effect at all unless the power says otherwise.                                                                                           |
| **Flags**        | Spell flags (**Conc**, **Channel**, **Ritual**, **Cast n**), and a recharge if it isn't unlimited (**SR** short rest, **LR** long rest, **Daily**).                                          |
| **Fits**         | Professions it suits, as a seeding hint only: War, Kni, Rog, Ran, Pal, Wlk, Wit, Wiz, Sor, Mag, Sum, Psy, plus "Any".                                                                        |

### Guardrails used while brainstorming

- Single-target damage rolls **Atk**. Anything with a condition, a debuff or an area rolls **Save** (GB-4).
- **Healing is never at-will.** Healing spells can be **rituals**.
- **Hard control** (stunned or stronger) is never at-will, never a ritual. Each condition respects its minimum tier and max duration (§12).
- Control longer than 1 turn is **save-ends**, with a cumulative +2 on each repeat save.
- Paralyzed and permanent petrification need **concentration**.
- Invisible lasts at most 2 turns in combat at T2, or 3 turns at T3+.
- Spell flags (Conc, Channel, Ritual, Cast) are only on spells.
- No creature types: nothing says "bonus against undead". Damage types and affinities do that job.
- Momentum traits follow the GB-20 table, including the round-1 rule.
- **Momentum buys actions only** (user decision, PQ-1). No power costs Momentum. The most a trait can do is let a power be used as a Minor Action instead of a Major Action.

## 1. Spells

Every spell belongs to one of **five schools**: Manifestation, Transmutation, Manipulation, Noetics and Vitalism. Conjuration and Warding were folded into the others (user decision, PQ-2). Summoning and binding spirits went to Vitalism, and protective spells went where their effect fits, mostly Manipulation. A power that moved keeps its original ID (`CON-n`, `WAR-n`) so earlier references still work.

**Batch 3 (2026-11-27, at the user's request):** new spells fill the gaps in knowledge and divination, light and dark, energy used for more than damage, creating matter, travel, and magic against magic (MAN-27 to MAN-54, TRA-35 to TRA-49, MNP-44 to MNP-59, NOE-51 to NOE-66, VIT-39). Necromancy got its own subsection (VIT-40 to VIT-65). Spells whose names clashed with D&D were renamed; IDs didn't change, and the old names are in the [rename log](#renamed-spells-2026-11-27). Not balanced.

#### Necromancy: binding the body and the spirit

Necromancy is part of Vitalism, which covers life and death (user decision, 2026-11-27). **To bind something is to use either its body or its spirit:**

- **Body binding** works on flesh and bone. On the living, it restrains them physically (shackles of bone, seized muscles, a body moved like a puppet). On the dead, it raises and drives the corpse.
- **Spirit binding** works on the soul, of the living or the dead: calling it, questioning it, tethering it, caging it or driving it out. The spirits table above is spirit binding by summoning.

Raised corpses are summons and use the same placeholder (`[PQ-3]`). There are no creature types, so nothing here says "undead": a raised corpse is a summon like any other. Body-binding spells need a corpse (or bones) in range.

## 3. Techniques

Techniques are the **main source of weapon damage**. A weapon offers a technique if it shares **at least one** property with it (light, heavy, finesse, reach, thrown, bladed, blunt, polearm, hafted, ranged, chained). Weapon riders add to techniques only. Damage type follows the weapon's form unless the technique says otherwise.

- **At-will** techniques are 1d8 at tier 1.
- **Costed** techniques mix MP and recharge (user decision, PQ-1). T1 costs 2–3 MP and T2 costs 5 MP. T3 has no cost but recharges on a short rest (**SR**); T4 has no cost but recharges on a long rest (**LR**). A few reckless techniques cost HP instead. Momentum never pays for a technique.
- "Range" is the weapon's reach (5 ft melee, or 10 ft with `reach`) unless the technique says otherwise. Ranged and thrown techniques use the range shown.
- At-wills for higher tiers are `[PQ-9]`.

### 4.6 Profession cores

The shared **core** of each profession (PROFESSION_CATALOG §3), granted `auto` at L1, L2, L5 and L9. Each trait's min level is the level it's granted. Path grants are in 4.7.

- **They're traits** because they're class grants. A trait used as an action shows it under **Act**; a passive shows "—". `grantedPowers` lists anything the trait hands over.
- **Class-only weapon moves are techniques** (they still need a weapon with a matching property), but a weapon offers them only to someone whose trait grants them. These are marked **trait-only**.
- **Fight-long resources** (Conviction, Debt, omens, links, doubles, the moon phase) last until the end of the fight, then reset. None has a model field yet, so they're description-only, like threat margin. None of them is Momentum, and none buys actions.
- Every number here is a first draft for the power balancer.
