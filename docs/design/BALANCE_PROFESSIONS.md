# RPGHub Profession Balancing

Balancing math for the numbers in [PROFESSION_CATALOG.md](PROFESSION_CATALOG.md). Findings here are not rule changes until the rules lead adopts them.

Part of the RPGHub design docs (index at the top of [GAME_RULES.md](GAME_RULES.md)). **§n** means a GAME_RULES section; **B-n** means a section of [BALANCE_ITEMS.md](BALANCE_ITEMS.md).

Target: **3–6 hits to down someone at every level.**

---

## Level-1 sanity math

- **Attack:** a Ranger (Accuracy 3) attacks a Rogue (Evasion 2). The target is 12, so the Ranger hits on a 9+ (60%).
- **Save:** a Witch (Dominance 3) hexes a Warrior (Resolve 1). The DC is 14, so the Warrior resists on a 13+ (40%).
- **Damage:** a Warrior technique deals 2d6 (avg 7), plus Might 3, minus a Knight's Resilience 3, for 7 per hit. The Knight's 30 HP lasts about 4 hits.

## Level-10 check

a level-10 tier-3 power (4d8 + Might 5, average 23) against Resilience 4 deals about 19 per hit. That's about 5 hits on a Warrior and 3 on a Psychic (60 HP), close to the level-1 numbers in section 2. A fully optimized technique build (Might 9, a tier-4 4d10 and two legendary riders, about 39) downs a Psychic in about 1.5 hits. That's the extreme case, and the reason tanks have protective traits (section 11).

## Related findings

Profession findings from the core balancing log, with rulings, are in [BALANCE_RULES.md](BALANCE_RULES.md):

- **GB-5:** tank professions get a protective trait at L1.
- **GB-7:** the L1 cap of 5 includes gear.
- **GB-14:** the species perk is +1 to two stats or +10 HP/MP.
- **GB-16:** every profession starts with at least 24 HP.
- **GB-21:** fragile professions at 2.2–2.7 hits; accepted as glass-cannon identity.
- **Pass 2 and pass 3:** hits-to-down tables per profession and level.
